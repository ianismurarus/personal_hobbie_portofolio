🎵 Music Theory

About Music Theory

Music theory is the language musicians use to communicate. It allows us to understand the structure, harmony, and progressions that make a piece of music sound cohesive. Through in-depth study of theory, I have gained the ability to write and perform music with much greater clarity.

Scales, intervals, and chord progressions are the building blocks of any composition. I have learned how to analyze songs to uncover the patterns that make them effective, and how to apply these concepts to my own musical ideas.

Understanding music theory doesn't limit creativity — on the contrary, it sets it free. With a solid theoretical foundation, I have far more freedom to experiment and make deliberate, intentional musical choices.

Core Principles Covered

  • Musical notes and standard notation
  • Scales and musical modes
  • Intervals and note relationships
  • Chord progressions and cadences
  • Rhythm, meter, and time signatures
